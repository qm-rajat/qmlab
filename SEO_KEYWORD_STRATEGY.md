## 1. Executive Strategy: High-Intent vs. Generic Head Terms

Single broad keywords like `"freelancer"` or `"software engineers"` have billions of search results and are dominated by multinational aggregators (Upwork, Fiverr, LinkedIn, Wikipedia) and educational dictionaries. 

**The Winning Formula:**
Instead of competing for low-intent, generic traffic, this strategy captures **High-Commercial-Intent Queries**—searches made by startup founders, agency owners, and hiring managers with budget in hand looking to contract a senior full-stack specialist.

```
Low Intent (Broad)                   High Intent (Commercial & Conversion)
-------------------                  -------------------------------------
"freelancer"            ----->       "hire freelance full-stack developer"
"software engineers"    ----->       "freelance software engineer for React Node.js"
"webservice"            ----->       "custom web development services for startups"
```
